              🧭
              

                
True North program

                
Completed the training · Program trainer

              

            

            
True North is a team-coaching program built around one idea: teams should move from uncertainty to evidence. Instead of polishing opinions, teams learn to test what they believe about their customers and let real behaviour decide what happens next.

            

              

                
What the program means

                
    
                  
  • Understand the customer first: empathy interviews, then zooming in on the one problem that matters most right now.
    • 
                  
  • Turn beliefs into testable assumptions, then run experiments that measure what people actually do, not what they say.
    • 
                  
  • Decide from evidence, and track progress with metrics that change what the team does next.
    • 
                  
  • Work in short sprints with planning, daily stand-ups and retrospectives, clear roles and open communication.
    • 
                

              

              

                
What it means for me

                
    
                  
  • I completed the training and then worked as a program trainer, helping others learn the method.
    • 
                  
  • It sharpened how I coach and communicate with teams: asking better questions and keeping everyone focused on shared goals.
    • 
                  
  • The same mindset drives my testing: assumptions are made explicit, and quality is judged by observed behaviour, not opinion.
    •
